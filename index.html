Who I am

About

Environmental scientist

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Gyeongkuk National University in Andong, South Korea, working in the lab of Prof. Chuleui Jung. I study how climate, land use and landscape structure shape biodiversity, species interactions and ecosystem functioning, from single fields to the whole globe.

Pollinators and food production

Much of my work concerns plant–pollinator interactions and the pollination services that support food production and human well-being. I combine field ecology, satellite remote sensing, geospatial analysis and species distribution modelling to address questions in pollination ecology, landscape ecology, biodiversity conservation and sustainable agriculture.

Current research

My current research uses drone-based remote sensing, computer vision and deep learning to detect, map and quantify flowering resources in forest and grassland ecosystems.

  • spinetv0.1.2Spatial Interaction Networks Under Environmental ChangeProjects ecological interaction networks through space and time from species distribution model output, with network metrics, interaction beta-diversity, rewiring and coextinction robustness.R package · github.com/ehsanrahimi666/spinet
  • InsectTrackv0.1.0Detection, Tracking and Activity Mapping of Insects in VideosDetects and tracks insects in videos with YOLO, open-vocabulary or background-subtraction detectors and turns their trajectories into counts, activity time series, heat maps, flower visits and nest traffic, with standard tracking-accuracy metrics.R package · github.com/ehsanrahimi666/InsectTrack
  • InsectTraitv0.1.0Colour, Pattern and Shape Traits of Insects from PhotographsExtracts 67 colour, pattern and shape traits of insects from field photographs or GBIF and iNaturalist observations, with photo screening, colour and exposure checks and trait reliability tests.R package · github.com/ehsanrahimi666/InsectTrait
  • FlowerTraitv0.1.0Colour, Pattern, Symmetry and Shape Traits of Flowers from PhotographsExtracts flower colour, pattern, symmetry and shape traits from field photographs or GBIF and iNaturalist observations, with photo quality screening and trait reliability checks.R package · github.com/ehsanrahimi666/FlowerTrait
  • svidv0.1.0Systematic Spectral Index Discovery, Deduplication and ValidationGenerates candidate spectral indices from multispectral or hyperspectral bands, removes duplicate formulations and ranks the rest under spatially aware validation with confidence intervals and reference baselines.R package · github.com/ehsanrahimi666/_SVID
